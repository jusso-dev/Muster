# Code of Conduct

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone.

Be constructive, assume good intent while verifying security claims, protect confidential information, and focus critique on work and outcomes. Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person’s sensitive information.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behaviour is unsafe or abusive. Report conduct concerns privately to `conduct@jusso.dev`. Reports will be handled confidentially and without retaliation.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. It is based on the principles of Contributor Covenant 2.1.
